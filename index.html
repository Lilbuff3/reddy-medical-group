PRACTICE OVERVIEW

Dedicated Care Built on Clinical Continuity

Reddy Medical Group provides compassionate, evidence-based adult medical care. Our physicians bridge the gap between outpatient preventative wellness and acute inpatient hospital management.

Adult Primary Care & Prevention

Comprehensive evaluation, diagnosis, and long-term management of chronic conditions including hypertension, type 2 diabetes, hyperlipidemia, and cardiovascular risk reduction.

Inpatient Hospitalist Medicine

Active attending privileges and inpatient hospitalist care at Saint Agnes Medical Center and Community Medical Centers, ensuring seamless care transitions from admission to discharge.

Infectious Disease Consultations

Board-certified subspecialty care for complex bacterial, viral, and fungal infections, post-operative surgical wound care, antimicrobial stewardship, and immunocompromised management.

Clinical Telehealth Options

Structured video and telephone follow-up appointments for stable chronic care reviews, routine lab consults, and medication monitoring when in-person clinic visits are not strictly required.
